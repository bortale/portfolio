About

Built for the messy middle of real organisations

I came to L&D through two decades of actually running things. Translation agencies, food truck operations, integration programmes. I've been the person responsible when systems break and people struggle.

That experience shaped how I think: performance problems are almost always design problems. And the fix is rarely more training — it's a better system around people.

I work across Ukrainian, Russian, English, and Swedish — which means I've built learning in contexts where language, culture, and capability all intersect at once. That's not a coincidence. It's a lens.

I'm now directing that experience fully toward L&D and organisational learning — and considering doctoral research on learning system design in complex multicultural environments.

How I Work

Approach

01

Find the real problem

I start with symptoms and work backwards. Rework, slow onboarding, disengagement — these are signals. I don't jump to solutions before understanding the system.

02

Design around people

I map capability and decision-making before designing learning. Not everyone needs the same thing — and behaviour doesn't change through information alone.

03

Embed learning in work

Micro-feedback, peer support, and error analysis become part of how work actually happens — not separate events people attend and forget.

04

Measure what matters

Onboarding speed. Rework rate. Retention. Decisions made. I connect learning outcomes to real results — not completion rates.
